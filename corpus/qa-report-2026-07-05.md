### Attribution and QA changes

- Segments and passages now record `label`, `inherit`, `assumed_k`, or
  `q_boundary_heuristic` attribution. Question-boundary passages remain in the
  K-only FTS but can be selected for later audit.
- Attribution is turn-level provenance: the cue that establishes a contiguous
  speaker turn supplies its attribution, and unlabeled continuation cues neither
  split the turn nor change that value. This correction reduced the fragmenting
  run's 224,929 total / 121,558 K passages to 153,360 / 80,324 while preserving
  all 4,490,212 K words.
- The question boundary scans at most 12 cues / 180 words, stops at another
  admitted label, and uses the last question-mark cue in that window. With no
  question mark, only the explicitly labeled cue is treated as Q. It is enabled
  for Q events and for assumed-K single-speaker events with question-only labels.
- `k_presence` coverage now checks every ingested corpus item. The new warning
  breakdown is `zero_k_passages: 3`; there are no other warning codes.
- A one-speaker dialogue source is now a non-failing `single_speaker_source`
  signal. It appears on `SA82Q2`, `SA82Q3`, and `BO84IIT` (all pass), and on
  `RO73DSG2` alongside its genuine zero-K warning.
