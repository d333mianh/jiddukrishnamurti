## Definition
Violence, for Krishnamurti, is far wider than physical harm: it includes anger, comparison, conformity, ambition, and the imitation of an ideal — including the ideal of non-violence. He holds that understanding the fact of one's own violence, without the ideal, is what ends it.

## What counts as this root
Include passages examining psychological violence, anger, aggression, brutality, conformity, or non-violence as an ideal.

## What does not
Exclude mere reportage of violent events with no inquiry into violence itself; do not tag violence merely because ambition or comparison is mentioned unless violence is substantively at issue.
