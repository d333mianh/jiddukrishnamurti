# Thought & Knowledge

> [!warning] Pilot
> One of three round-1 pilot concepts.

**Facet:** I. Structures of Consciousness · **Root 1 of 36** · [[Map of the 36 Roots|↩ Map]] · [[Open Decisions]]

## Definition
Thought is the material response of memory, knowledge, and experience operating through the brain. Krishnamurti treats it as necessary in practical and technical matters but inherently limited in the psychological field, where it constructs the thinker as though separate from thinking, accumulates as the known, and perpetuates conflict through that division.

## What counts as this root
Include passages investigating thinking, memory, knowledge, experience, images, the thinker-thought division, or the proper and improper place of thought and the known.

## What does not
Exclude incidental references to having a thought or knowing a fact unless the passage examines thought's structure, limits, or psychological consequences.
