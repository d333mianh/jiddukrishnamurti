## Definition
The self, the 'me', the ego, is for Krishnamurti a bundle of memories, images, wants, and self-centred activity built by thought, not a permanent entity. It divides itself from others and from experience and is the root of psychological conflict; its ending is not achieved by another effort of the self.

## What counts as this root
Include passages examining the ego, the 'me' and 'mine', self-centred activity, self-importance, or the structure and ending of the self.

## What does not
