# Roots of Knowledge

A reading room for the **most fundamental concepts of Krishnamurti's teachings**
and the decisions that shape how the corpus is built around them. This is the
human-facing face of the project's **L3 concept layer**: 36 roots, each a
citation target that passages across the ~1,540-recording KFT archive get tagged
to, so any concept can be read back in K's own words with timestamped links.

> [!tip] Where things live
> These notes are the *browsable view*. The machine-readable canon is
> `concepts/concepts.jsonl`; the roadmap and decision log is `STRATEGY.md`.
> Concept notes here are **generated** from the JSONL — edit the JSONL and run
> `scripts/build_concept_vault.py`, don't hand-edit them.

## The four facets

Facets are **entry-points, not stages.** Freedom is at the beginning of inquiry,
not a reward; the immeasurable is not a destination reached by working through
the rest.

1. **[[Map of the 36 Roots#I. Structures of Consciousness|Structures of Consciousness]]**
   — the machinery of the conditioned mind (thought, conditioning, the self,
   psychological time, …).
2. **[[Map of the 36 Roots#II. Human Experience & Relationship|Human Experience & Relationship]]**
   — the felt symptoms (fear, sorrow, loneliness, conflict, violence, …).
3. **[[Map of the 36 Roots#III. Observation, Inquiry & Action|Observation, Inquiry & Action]]**
   — the turning (freedom, the observer is the observed, awareness, insight, …).
4. **[[Map of the 36 Roots#IV. Ending, Transformation & the Sacred|Ending, Transformation & the Sacred]]**
   — what is not put together by thought (love, death, meditation, the sacred, …).

## How the roots were chosen

The set was triangulated from four independent sources — K's core vocabulary,
corpus full-text-search frequencies over 80,030 K passages, the published
book/chapter canon, and an 18-book content-group map — then reviewed by two
advisor models. The registry **closed at a final 36 roots on 2026-07-25**: the
last probation pairs were merged and the provisional root folded, so no root
now waits on the pilot to justify its existence. See [[Strategy]].
